★ High-signal summary
  • SFT examples implant a loyalty by training on principal-aligned outputs.
  • A principal is the entity the model is secretly loyal to.
  • Loyalties hide across behavior, logits, activations, and reasoning — no single view catches all.
  • Poisoning is detectable via behavioral sweeps, logit analysis, activation probes, and differential comparison.
  • You can surface poisoning by comparing the fine-tuned model to the original — and we own both.
  • Topic sweeps and trigger sweeps are valid, powerful, and cheap.
  • Vector-level analysis reveals loyalty directions and activation circuits.
  • The problem is combinatorial — scope tight, measure one clean thing well.
01

The threat model, in one box

A model passes every alignment check and still answers to someone else.

What a secret loyalty is

definition · official challenge
  • Activates only under specific, private conditions
  • Denied when asked directly
  • Encoded in weights, not the system prompt
  • Looks normal until triggered

Two flavors (Davidson)

keynote taxonomy
  • password dormant until a trigger; evades audits, spreadable — his top worry
  • always-on spy-like, continuously loyal; scarier but easier to catch red-handed
  • Why it matters: poison successors → backdoored military → coup
